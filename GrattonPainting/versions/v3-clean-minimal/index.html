Services

Painting and finish work for homes and small business.

Every job opens with a walkthrough, so the scope, the products, and the price are settled long before a can is opened.

Interior Painting

Walls, ceilings, trim, doors, and accent walls in low-odor products, with furniture and floors covered top to bottom.

Exterior Painting

Stucco, siding, and trim finished in UV- and moisture-resistant systems selected for salt air and coastal sun.

Cabinet Refinishing

Kitchen and bath cabinetry degreased, sanded, and sprayed for a smooth, factory-style finish without a full remodel.

Pressure Washing

Driveways, walkways, lanais, and exteriors cleared of mildew, algae, and salt — on their own or as prep for paint.

Drywall & Repairs

Patching, texture matching, caulking, and rot repair so every finish coat lands on a sound, solid surface.

Commercial & Rentals

Offices, storefronts, condos, and unit turnovers scheduled around your tenants, your staff, and your business hours.

Process

Four steps, start to finish.

No moving targets and no surprise add-ons. You know what happens next at every stage of the project.

  1. 01

    Walkthrough

    We look at the space with you, talk through goals and colors, then measure.

  2. 02

    Written quote

    A clear written scope and a fixed price, itemized so nothing is left vague.

  3. 03

    Prep & paint

    Cover, repair, prime, and coat — with the site tidied at the end of each day.

  4. 04

    Final walk

    We inspect the work together and correct anything before we call it done.
